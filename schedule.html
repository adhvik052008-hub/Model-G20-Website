13 – 15 November 2026 · FLAME University, Pune

Summit/Schedule

Three days, hour by hour

Six committee sessions, one unannounced crisis, one live press conference and a closing plenary that transmits every outcome document to the Leaders' Summit.

All times are India Standard Time (UTC+5:30). The Chair may extend or compress a session on the floor; the Emergency Sherpa Session convenes at the Chair's discretion and its listed time is indicative only.

At a glance

What each day asks of you

Friday 13 November

Arrive prepared

Bring your position paper, printed. Opening statements are ninety seconds and the Chair will hold you to it. By the end of day one your committee should have an agreed agenda and you should know who your bloc is.

Sessions I – II
Saturday 14 November

Draft, merge, defend

The longest day. Working papers become draft outcome documents, blocs consolidate, and the crisis will interrupt whatever you had planned. The press will quote you at lunch.

Sessions III – V + Crisis
Sunday 15 November

Land it

Final amendments, then adoption. A document that fails to reach consensus is recorded as failed and transmitted as such — the Leaders' Summit is told what you could not agree.

Session VI + Plenary
On the floor

Dress & conduct

Expected
  • Western business formal in all sessions and the closing plenary
  • National or traditional formal attire, equally welcome and encouraged for the opening ceremony
  • Placard raised to be recognised; never speak before the Chair recognises you
  • Third person throughout formal debate — the delegate, not I
  • Position paper printed and submitted before the first session
Not permitted
  • Laptops and phones during formal debate; permitted in unmoderated caucus and drafting
  • Leaving committee without the Chair's leave once a session is in progress
  • Pre-written outcome documents brought in from outside
  • Attributing a position to a delegation that has not taken it
  • Anything that would not survive being printed in the morning edition
Before you arrive

Everything you need to read

Delegate handbook, rules of procedure, your committee's background guide and the position paper guidelines.
