Eight committees

The committees

Every committee sits at the same table. Each has one agenda, debated across both days, and each is graded so you can pick the room that matches your experience rather than the one with the longest title.

Beginner — no prior experience assumed Intermediate — some committee experience helps Advanced — for practised delegates

Agendas are final. Background guides and rules of procedure are issued to every allotted delegate ahead of the conference.

Found your committee?

One form. One submission.

Registration takes a few minutes. You name a committee preference on the form — allotments are confirmed by the Secretariat afterwards.
