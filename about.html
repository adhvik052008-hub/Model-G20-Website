What is the G20?

The beginning of the world's biggest decisions.

The Group of Twenty (G20) is an international forum that brings together the world's major economies to discuss and address global challenges. The forum includes 19 countries along with the African Union and the European Union, making it one of the most representative platforms for global decision-making.

A Model G20 aims to recreate the unique environment of international diplomacy. Participants represent G20 member nations and work together to address real-world global issues through negotiation, collaboration, policy-making, and strategic problem-solving.

02

Why a Model G20?

The world doesn't need more debates. It needs solutions.

Model United Nations focuses on debate and resolution writing, while Model G20 focuses on consensus based decision making. Delegates do more than represent their country, they negotiate, collaborate, and build agreements that lead to practical solutions.

What makes our Model G20 unique?

Whether you are an experienced delegate or stepping into international diplomacy for the first time, The School of Raya offers an opportunity to think beyond borders, challenge perspectives, and contribute to conversations that shape the future.
