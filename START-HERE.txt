THE SCHOOL OF RAYA MODEL G20 2026 — COMPLETE PROJECT
====================================================
10th – 11th October 2026 · The School of Raya, Bengaluru

This archive is the whole project: the finished website, the source it is
built from, the build script, the images and the documentation. Nothing is
missing and nothing needs installing.


I JUST WANT IT LIVE
-------------------
Zip this folder and drag it onto  https://app.netlify.com/drop
Full instructions, including how to UPDATE a site without creating a new
one each time, are in  HOW-TO-DEPLOY.txt


I WANT TO SEE IT ON MY OWN MACHINE FIRST
----------------------------------------
Open a terminal in this folder and run:

    python3 -m http.server 8000

Then visit  http://localhost:8000
Press Ctrl+C when you are done.

(Double-clicking index.html mostly works, but the committee cards and team
cards stay blank — browsers block those scripts on local files.)


I WANT TO CHANGE SOMETHING
--------------------------
Read  EDITING.md  — it is written for someone who has never opened this
project before and assumes no prior knowledge.

The one rule: edit the files inside  src/  , never the .html files sitting
in this top folder. Those are generated. After editing, run:

    python3 build.py

That rebuilds the pages in about a second. If you forget and edit a
generated file by mistake, the build notices and refuses to overwrite your
work rather than silently losing it.

A NOTE ON COLOUR
----------------
The site uses exactly four colours and no others:

    #EFDDC6  beige, the page ground
    #600808  deep maroon, the accent
    #FFFFFF  white, card surfaces
    #000000  black, type

Every tint, rule and shadow is one of those four at reduced opacity. If you
add anything, keep to them. The partner logos carry their own brand colours,
which is correct — a logo is never repainted to match a site.

Do not swap in white versions of the logos. The background is beige and the
cards are white, so white artwork disappears.
