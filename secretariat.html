Who convenes it

The Secretariat

Every conference is only as good as the people who hold the room. These are the offices that convene School of Raya Model G20 2026, what each one is answerable for, and how to reach them.

Core Secretariat

Substantive authority

The Secretariat owns the academic standard of the conference: the agendas, the Chair's bench, the background guides and the conduct of the floor. Appointments are announced ahead of the conference.

Organising Committee

Everything else

Registration, logistics, press, design, outreach and technology. If it happens on the day and it is not a debate, one of these offices owns it.
