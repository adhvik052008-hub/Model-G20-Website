01

Principles

Paper, not screen

The ground is parchment with a real grain, not a flat hex fill. Rules are hairlines. Shadows are warm, never neutral grey. The reference is a printed communiqué, not a dashboard.

Maroon is a signature, not a surface

#600808 marks decisions: the primary action, the active state, the rule under a live link. Whole sections go maroon only to mark a change in register — the theme statement, a call to act, the footer.

Typography carries the hierarchy

Two families, three jobs. A high-contrast serif for anything with gravitas, a neo-grotesque for anything with data, and small caps at wide tracking for the labels between them. Weight does less work than scale.

Motion explains, never entertains

Reveals establish reading order. Sweeps confirm a target. Arcs on the map show convergence. Everything obeys prefers-reduced-motion without degrading meaning.

Elevation & glass

Shadows are tinted with maroon, never neutral. Glass is used for anything that floats over content: the navigation bar, the hero panel, the map's information card and the matrix toolbar.

--sh-xs

Hairline lift. Chips and inline plates.

--sh-sm

Resting state for cards and tables.

--sh-md

Hover on secondary surfaces.

--sh-lg

Hover on interactive cards, dropdowns.

--sh-xl

Modals and the lightbox only.

Glass — default

45% ivory, 22px blur, 165% saturation, hairline inset highlight. The navigation bar and hero panel.

Glass — strong

80% ivory. Used where text must stay legible over busy content — the map information card, dropdown panels.

06

Motion

Four easings, six durations. Anything that moves does so to explain where something came from or where it is going.

Easing
--e-outcubic-bezier(.22, 1, .36, 1) — default for state change
--e-out-expocubic-bezier(.16, 1, .30, 1) — reveals, sweeps, drawers
--e-in-outcubic-bezier(.65, 0, .35, 1) — looping ambience
--e-springcubic-bezier(.34, 1.56, .64, 1) — checkboxes, pins, lift
Duration
--d-instant · 90msColour-only changes
--d-fast · 180msHover, focus, small toggles
--d-base · 320msPanels, dropdowns, modal fade
--d-slow · 600msCard lift, accordions, button sweep
--d-slower · 980msScroll reveal
--d-cine · 1400msMasked heading reveal, curtain lift
Under prefers-reduced-motion: reduce every transition collapses to 0.001ms, reveals resolve to their final state, the paper grain is removed, the custom cursor is not mounted and the map draws its arcs statically. No content is lost.

08

Identity

A circular seal: twenty dots on the outer ring — one for each member of the table — around a meridian globe. The dots are the point of the mark, and the reason it is never simplified further.

Do
  • Reproduce in a single colour — maroon on parchment, or parchment on maroon
  • Keep clear space of at least the outer ring's radius on all sides
  • Minimum size 28px; below that use the globe alone without the dot ring
Don't
  • Re-colour, gradient-fill or add a drop shadow to the seal
  • Change the count of ring dots or their spacing
  • Stretch, rotate or place the mark on a photograph without a scrim

09

Accessibility

The standard the build is held to, and how each item is met.

Contrast

Maroon #600808 on parchment #EFDDC6 measures approximately 9.4:1 — comfortably past AAA for body text. Reversed, parchment on maroon-800 measures approximately 11:1. No text-bearing pair in the system falls below 4.5:1.

Keyboard

Every interactive element is reachable and operable by keyboard, including the map's delegation pins, which are focusable buttons with labels. Modals trap focus and restore it to the opener on close. A skip link precedes the navigation.

Announcement

The countdown announces politely on the day boundary rather than every second. The map's information card is a live region. Tabs, accordions and the drawer carry the expected ARIA state and respond to arrow keys.

Without JavaScript

Reveal animations are applied only under html.js, so a visitor without JavaScript sees fully rendered content rather than an empty page. Navigation, layout and every static page section work unchanged.

Implementation

Consuming the system

Components read semantic tokens, never raw values. That is what makes section inversion a one-class change.

Correct 
/* reads the context it is dropped into */
.my-panel {
  background: var(--surface);
  color: var(--text-body);
  border: 1px solid var(--line);
  border-radius: var(--r-lg);
  box-shadow: var(--sh-sm);
  padding: var(--sp-6);
}
Incorrect 
/* breaks the moment it enters .on-dark */
.my-panel {
  background: #FFFFFF;
  color: #333333;
  border: 1px solid #e5e5e5;
  border-radius: 20px;
  box-shadow: 0 2px 6px rgba(0,0,0,.08);
  padding: 30px;
}
