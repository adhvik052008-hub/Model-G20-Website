The Secretariat

Twelve offices run this summit. Each one is accountable for something a delegate will notice if it fails — and for nothing that overlaps with another office.

Structure

Student-led, faculty-supervised

The Secretariat is appointed from the student body of The School of Raya and supervised by faculty on behalf of the host institution.

Authority runs from the Secretary-General through the Director-General for everything substantive — committees, chairs, background guides, awards — and through the Deputy Secretary-General for everything administrative. The Chargé d'Affaires sits deliberately outside that chain: a delegate who cannot get an answer from their own committee bench writes to the Chargé, and the Chargé answers.

Every chair is trained and certified before summit. Every background guide is reviewed by the Office of Committee Affairs before release. Award decisions rest with the committee bench and are not reviewed by the Secretariat.

The offices

Who is accountable for what

Appointments for the inaugural edition are announced by the host ahead of summit. The remit of each office is fixed regardless of who holds it.

The bench

Chairs & the committee bench

Each committee is run by a bench of three: a Chair, a Vice-Chair and a Rapporteur. The Chair holds the floor and rules on procedure. The Vice-Chair runs the speakers' list and manages caucus. The Rapporteur keeps the record and is responsible for the committee's outcome document reaching the Leaders' Summit intact.

Benches are appointed by the Office of Committee Affairs following an open application and a procedural examination. Applications for the 2026 bench open alongside delegate registration.

Standing rule

One bench, one committee

No member of the Secretariat sits on a committee bench for a committee in which their own institution holds a delegation. The rule is absolute and there is no waiver.

Standing rule

Awards are not reviewed

The Chair's assessment of a committee's awards is final. The Secretariat does not review, moderate or overturn it, and will not discuss individual placements after the ceremony.

Standing rule

Safeguarding

The Faculty Advisor holds safeguarding responsibility for the duration of the conference and may suspend any session. Concerns should be raised with the Faculty Advisor or the Chargé d'Affaires directly.
