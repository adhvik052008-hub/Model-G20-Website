How allotment works

Four things we weigh

01 — Preference order

What you asked for

You rank three committees and three countries. We start at the top of both lists and work down. The overwhelming majority of applicants receive one of their first two committee choices.

02 — Experience

Whether the seat fits

Advanced committees weigh stated experience. This is not a filter on who may attend — it is a check that a first-time delegate is not placed in FMCBG on day one without support.

03 — Delegation balance

Spreading a delegation

Institutional delegations are distributed across committees rather than concentrated in one. A delegation of eight will not receive eight seats in the same room.

04 — Date completed

When you finished

Ties break on the date the registration was completed, not started. A half-finished form does not hold a seat.
