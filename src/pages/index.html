Conference Timeline

Key dates

From the day registration opens to the opening gavel. Allotted delegates are notified by email at every step.

  1. 1st August 2026 Registration opens Applications accepted from schools and individual delegates.
  2. 15th September 2026 Registration closes Late applications are considered only against unfilled seats.
  3. 22nd September 2026 Allotments released Committee and country allocations issued by email.
  4. 25th September 2026 Background guides issued Agenda briefs and rules of procedure sent to every delegate.
  5. 8th – 9th October 2026 The conference Two days at The School of Raya, Bengaluru.
