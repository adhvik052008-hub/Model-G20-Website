Information architecture

Eleven destinations, three tiers

Primary navigation carries the six destinations a prospective delegate needs before deciding. Secondary destinations sit behind the overflow menu. Registration is never in either — it is a persistent action.

Tier 1 — Primary nav

Deciding

About · Committees · Country Matrix · Schedule · Resources · Gallery

Everything needed to answer “should I apply, and to what?”

Tier 2 — Overflow

Verifying

Secretariat · FAQs · Contact · Design System

Consulted once, usually late, usually by a faculty advisor or a partner.

Tier 3 — Persistent action

Converting

Registration

Present in the bar at every scroll position, in every CTA band, and in the footer.

Four user journeys

Every page exists to serve at least one of these. A page that serves none does not ship.

01 — The prospective delegate

LandHomeCountdown and theme establish scale in under five seconds.
AssessCommitteesDifficulty grading answers “is this for me?” before the agenda does.
CheckCountry MatrixFilter to open seats in the committee they want.
ConfirmFAQsFee, refund policy, dress code, deadline.
ConvertRegistrationFive steps, draft saved, deep-linked committee pre-filled.

02 — The faculty advisor

LandAboutLegitimacy first: who convenes it, where, under what supervision.
VerifySecretariatAccountability, safeguarding and the standing rules.
PlanScheduleThree-day programme, dress code, conduct expectations.
CostRegistrationDelegation rate, accommodation, advisor pass.
AskContactRouted to the right desk with a named response time.

03 — The allotted delegate, preparing

ReturnResourcesHandbook, rules of procedure, their committee's guide.
WritePosition papersStructure, format, plagiarism policy, the missed-deadline rule.
AimAwards criteriaFour weighted criteria, published in advance.
ArriveScheduleHour by hour, with accreditation and the crisis caveat.

04 — The partner or sponsor

ScaleHomeDelegates, committees, delegations, days — above the fold.
ProofGalleryThe visual record and the Press Corps' reach.
TermsResourcesSponsorship prospectus and the brand kit.
OpenContactPartnerships desk, direct.
Edge cases

States the design accounts for

Empty

Matrix search with no matches renders a mark, an explanation and a one-click reset — never a blank region.

Loading

The curtain holds for 900ms then lifts unconditionally, so a slow asset can never trap a visitor behind it.

Error

Field errors render inline from native validity messages, tint the control, and move focus to the first offender.

Success

Forms replace themselves with a confirmation panel in place, moving focus to its heading rather than scrolling silently.

Interrupted

Registration drafts persist to localStorage on every change and are restored with a visible notice.

Post-event

Once the start date passes, the countdown swaps its supporting copy to the in-progress message automatically.

Webfonts blocked

Both families declare full local fallback stacks, so the page keeps its serif/grotesque contrast offline.

Not found

A dedicated 404 keeps the full navigation and routes to the four most likely destinations.
